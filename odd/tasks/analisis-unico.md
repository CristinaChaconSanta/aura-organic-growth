# Un solo análisis por sitio

## Objective
Diagnosis and the SEO audit are one commercial record per site. Empty title, empty meta description, empty H1, and a broken sales page are observed opportunities. Maturity does not discard a lead. An incomplete inventory does not remove the client.

## Problem
Two pipelines (`correr_lote` and `auditoria_seo`) and the maturity gate dropped businesses that need the service. A measured empty title or a broken sales page was left as «no determinable», or the lead never reached the audit.

## Scope (authorized by Cristina, 2026-10-06)
- Opportunity rules on pages the crawl (advertools) or the homepage fetch actually measured.
- 429, 503, and no response stay «no determinable». They are not a broken page and not an invented absence.
- Maturity is recorded. Every lead with a web is analyzed. No web still routes to a landing.
- Incomplete sitemap coverage keeps the client. Uncrawled claims stay unstated. Measured gaps stay.
- `analisis.unir` is the one record.
